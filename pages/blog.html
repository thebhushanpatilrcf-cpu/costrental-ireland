30 July 2026 New Scheme

LDA Opens Applications for Cookstown Gateway, Dublin 24

The Land Development Agency has opened applications for a new batch of one-bedroom apartments at Cookstown Gateway in Dublin 24. Rents start at €1,225 per month — approximately 44% below current market rates in the area.

The development overlooks Cookstown Luas Stop on the Red Line, with walking distance access to Tallaght University Hospital and The Square Shopping Centre. It's also near Citywest Business Campus and Grange Castle Business Park.

Key Details

  • Rent: From €1,225/month
  • Bedrooms: 1 Bed apartments
  • Parking: Not available
  • Market comparison: Similar 1-beds in Dublin 24 average €2,100/month on Daft.ie
  • Saving: €875/month or €10,500/year

How to Apply

Register your interest on the LDA website. Applications close at 12:30 on Thursday 6 August 2026. A random lottery will determine allocation.

Eligibility: Net household income below €66,000. Not receiving HAP. Do not own property. Must be Irish resident.

25 July 2026 Tips

5 Tips to Maximize Your Chances in the Cost Rental Lottery

  1. Apply for every scheme you qualify for. There's no penalty and no limit on how many you can enter. More entries = higher overall odds.
  2. Have documents ready before you apply. If you win the lottery, you have just 72 hours to upload everything. Prepare your P60, payslips, landlord reference, and bank statements in advance.
  3. Set up notifications. Application windows are typically only 1-2 weeks. By the time you hear about it through word of mouth, it may be too late. Sign up for alerts here.
  4. Check your eligibility carefully. The 35% affordability test catches many applicants off guard. If your rent would exceed 35% of net income, you may be rejected even if you're under the income cap.
  5. Don't ignore schemes outside your preferred area. Less popular locations (Limerick, Kildare) have lower competition. You can always transfer later if another scheme opens closer to where you want to be.
