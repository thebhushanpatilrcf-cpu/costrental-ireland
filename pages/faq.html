Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about cost rental housing in Ireland.

Cost rental is a new housing tenure created under the Affordable Housing Act 2021. Rent is based on the cost of building, managing, and maintaining the homes — not market rates. This means rents are typically 25-40% below private market rent. It offers long-term, secure tenancies on a not-for-profit basis.

  • Net household income below €66,000 (Dublin) or €59,000 (elsewhere)
  • Do not own a property in Ireland or elsewhere
  • Not receiving social housing supports (HAP, Rent Supplement)
  • Permanent resident and tax domiciled in Ireland
  • Household size matches property size
  • Can afford rent (max 35% of net income)

Prices vary by location and type:

  • Dublin 1-bed: €1,020 - €1,300/month
  • Dublin 2-bed: €1,300 - €1,600/month
  • Dublin 3-bed: €1,500 - €1,700/month
  • Limerick 1-bed: from €990/month
  • Kildare 1-bed: from €1,357/month

All rents must be at least 25% below comparable market rates.

  1. Find an open scheme (check our listings page)
  2. Register interest on the provider's website
  3. Enter your details and verify eligibility
  4. If oversubscribed, a random lottery determines allocation
  5. If selected, upload documents within 72 hours
  6. Complete verification and receive your offer

Net income = gross income minus tax, USC, PRSI, and superannuation for all household members aged 18+.

Included: employment income, self-employment, pensions, social welfare payments, maintenance received.

Excluded: child benefit, carer's allowance, fuel allowance, foster care payments, scholarships.

After 6 months, you have a tenancy of unlimited duration. You can stay as long as you comply with your lease obligations. Cost rental is designed as a long-term home, not temporary accommodation.

Eligibility is assessed at point of entry only. If your income later exceeds the threshold, you can still stay. There is no ongoing income assessment.
